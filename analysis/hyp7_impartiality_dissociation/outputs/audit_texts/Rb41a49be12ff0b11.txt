

My estimate: **19,800,000**

Justification:

- **Giraffe population**: There are approximately 117,000 giraffes in the wild as of recent estimates (Giraffe Conservation Foundation, 2021).

- **Spots per giraffe**: While this varies by species and individual, a gross estimate might be around 200-300 spots per giraffe. Let's use an average of 250 spots per giraffe as a rough estimate.

- **Calculation**: 117,000 giraffes × 250 spots = 29,250,000 spots.

However, this might overestimate if spot counts trend lower (some giraffes, like the reticulated giraffe, have fewer, larger spots). Conversely, the Rothschild's giraffe has many smaller spots. To err on the side of caution and simplicity:

117,000 giraffes × 170 average spots = **19,890,000 spots**.

So my point estimate is **19,800,000**.
